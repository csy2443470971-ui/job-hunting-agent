# Role lenses

A lens decides which material leads and which vocabulary the page speaks. Résumé lines in 02 and 03 are tagged with the
lenses they serve; pick tagged lines in the order below, then fill with the strongest remaining ones. Headline titles and per-entry
titles per lens are set in code.

| Lens (tag) | Target titles | Leads with | The field's vocabulary | Guard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI product (`ai_product`) | AI Product Designer, Product Designer (AI), AI Experience Designer, Founding Designer | (your entries and projects) | multi-agent orchestration, human-in-the-loop, retrieval-augmented generation, evaluation, agent reasoning, model routing | none |
| Product design (`product_design`) | Product Designer, UX Designer, Interaction Designer | (your entries and projects) | information architecture, user flows, design system, heuristic evaluation, usability testing, high-fidelity prototyping | skip 5+ year senior roles |
| UX research (`ux_research`) | UX Researcher, Design Researcher, User Researcher | (your entries and projects) | mixed methods, contextual inquiry, Jobs-to-be-Done, synthesis, personas, journey maps | none |
| Design engineer (`design_engineer`) | Design Engineer, Creative Technologist, UX Engineer, Prototyper | (your entries and projects) | production HTML/CSS/JS, Three.js, WebGL, headless rendering, MCP, CLI tooling, Playwright | skip 6+ year front-end engineering roles |
| Digital art director (`art_director`) | Digital Art Director, Art Director (digital/interactive), Visual Designer, Brand Designer, Web Designer | (your entries and projects) | art direction, brand identity, visual identity system, typography, layout, motion language, campaign assets | cap senior/ACD campaign roles at 50 |
| Motion (`motion`) | Motion Designer (web/product), Interaction Designer, Creative Developer, Immersive Designer | (your entries and projects) | motion graphics, real-time rendering, particle systems, gesture interaction, scroll-driven animation | skip After Effects / C4D reel roles |
| Design strategy (`strategy`) | Design Strategist, Innovation Consultant, Service Designer, Strategy Designer | (your entries and projects) | innovation consulting, service blueprint, ecosystem mapping, opportunity framing, roadmap, market landscape | none |
| Brand strategy (`brand_strategy`) | Brand Strategist, Creative Strategist, Content Strategist, Marketing Designer | (your entries and projects) | brand positioning, messaging architecture, narrative, brand system, competitive audit | none |
| Founder's office / BD (`founders_office`) | Founder's Associate, Chief of Staff (early stage), BD or Partnerships Associate, Strategy & Operations Associate | (your entries and projects) | go-to-market, ideal customer profile, account mapping, offer packaging, pipeline, fundraising | early stage only; skip quota sales roles |

**Sector overlays**
- Climate (`climate` tag): climate entries move to the top.
- Agencies and studios: frame each line as constraint, decision, and what you made.
